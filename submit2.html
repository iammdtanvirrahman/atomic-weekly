Open Editorial Desk

Choose how
to submit.

Pick one of the three submission methods. Written articles enter the editorial queue through Firebase. Images and other formats are sent separately to the editorial Gmail.

Three simple options
01 Article Only · 02 Article with Image · 03 Other Format
01 · Article Only

Article Only

Send only your written article. It will appear in the Admin Panel as a pending submission for editorial review.

02 · Article + Image

Article with Image

Submit the article and cover image. The cover is uploaded to Firebase Storage and linked to the submission, then Gmail opens so you can also send the original image to the editor.

03 · Other Format

Other Format

For PDF, DOCX or another document format, use Gmail. The website prepares the email; you attach the file yourself before sending.

Gmail cannot receive a local browser file automatically. After Gmail opens, click the paperclip and select your file.
